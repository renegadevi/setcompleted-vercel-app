See the work your way

Stay on the keyboard

Name a project, tab into a list, then keep hitting Enter. Sections and the next task stay under your fingers — no modal for every row.

Workspaces and projects

Keep work, private, and client spaces apart. Projects live inside a workspace and hold the lists you actually open each day.

Due dates, priority, and status

Give a task a start date, a due date, and a status. Then you can see what is next, overdue, or already done.

List, board, calendar, and Gantt

Switch views on the same list. The last one you used is remembered, so you do not set it up again every time.

Share a list

Publish a read-only page for a list. Anyone with the link can follow the work without signing in.

Roadmap

Place work on Now, Next, and Later when an idea is bigger than a task, but still needs a home on the project.

Custom columns

Add text, number, date, or labeled fields when a list needs more than priority and status.

Dashboard and milestones

An overview of open work across the workspace, plus milestone dates when a deadline actually matters.

Drag and drop

Reorder tasks, move them between sections, or drop them on another day in the calendar and timeline.

Import, export, and backup

Bring data in, take it out, or keep a backup of a workspace when you want a copy you control.
